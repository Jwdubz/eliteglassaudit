I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a homeowner shopping for a shower door, or a contractor needing storefront glass, would. Checked October 16, 2026

What I found

01

A job ad sits in your customer homepage.

Right after your intro paragraph, the homepage text carries a hiring notice (“We are looking for: Experienced Commercial Glazier”). It appears three times in the page, with typos (“Commerical”, “enthusiatic”). A homeowner looking for a shower door reads a job posting first.

02

Most of your 1,478 sitemap pages are near-copies.

1,440 of the addresses are service-by-town pages across 72 service names and towns in Nevada, Utah and Arizona, such as /barn-door-shower/beaver-ut. Of 12 I opened at random, 3 carry the same generic title (“Glass Services | Clark County, NV | 702-440-8700”) and the same generic description. The sitemap also lists doubled addresses such as shower-repair and shower-repair76a8, and one wine-cellar page has the placeholder heading “Keyword”.

03

Quote requests come in two different ways.

The homepage has a small form (name, phone, email, message). The “Free Quote” buttons go to /quote, which loads a separate Cognito Forms form. The contact emails listed (miguel@ and raina@elitegmi.com) are on a different domain than the site. I didn't submit either form, so it's worth checking that both reach you.

04

Your homepage title doesn't say what you do.

The title is “Elite Glass & Mirror Inc. | Clark County, NV”, with no shower doors, mirrors or “Las Vegas” in it, and the homepage headline is “GLASS SERVICES”. The Gallery page has no top-level headline at all.

05

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

Three headings are out of order, and the two email links in the footer are very small tap targets.

What's working

The homepage loads fast: in a simulated slow-connection load test its main content appeared at 1.4 seconds. Your business markup carries your address, phone, map location and hours (Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 4:00), and the hours match the page. Every page I opened has a Call Now link, a map and a Free Quote button. For your own name you were the first result in my search.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), and whether your two quote forms reach you. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt allows every page and adds a line saying AI search and AI training are both allowed (Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=yes). I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Found
    Structured data describes the business

    The homepage includes LocalBusiness markup with name, address (4535 Statz St), phone (702-440-8700), map location, hours and an Instagram and Yelp link.

  • Found
    You publish an llms.txt file

    /llms.txt exists and lists 1,480 pages. Most are named only by town, like “beaver-ut”. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read.

  • Gap
    Near-copy pages

    1,440 of the 1,478 sitemap addresses are service-by-town pages. 3 of the 12 I opened share one generic title and description.

  • Gap
    Hiring notice in the homepage text

    “Experienced Commercial Glazier” appears three times in the homepage.

  • Note
    Two quote forms

    A form on the homepage and a Cognito Forms form on /quote.

  • Found
    Google listing links

    Your pages embed your Google map and link your Google Maps place and a Yelp page.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 16, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI answers say when asked directly. I didn't ask those assistants myself, so the searches above are a stand-in.
  • How many people search for these terms or how many visit your site.
  • Your Google listing itself: its reviews, photos, hours and phone number as Google shows them.
  • How your site looks and loads on a phone. I did not test a phone, so mobile is untested.

What I'd fix first

Move the job posting off the homepage and fix its typos, give every service-by-town page its own title and description (or fewer pages), put what you do and where in the homepage title, and decide which quote form is the main one. I can't promise where it will place you.
